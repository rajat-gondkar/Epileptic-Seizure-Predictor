WHAT THIS PROJECT IS (the 20-second pitch)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Most seizure-prediction tools look only at brain signals (EEG) and treat every
patient the same. But epilepsy has a strong genetic component. Our project
combines TWO sources of information:

  1. EEG  - the patient's live brain-wave recording
  2. Genetics - the patient's epilepsy-related gene profile

We built an AI model that FUSES both into a single, personalised seizure-risk
score, and then turns that score into a simple 4-level clinical alert
(Low / Moderate / High / Critical).

The dashboard you are showing is the visual story of the whole system - from
raw data, to the models, to the final fused prediction, plus a live demo where
a patient file is analysed in front of the audience.

WHY:  This is the proof that the system actually does something useful on a
real patient case, not just charts. It is the most convincing part of the demo.

IMPORTANT POINT TO MAKE - why genetic risk is constant:
  Genetics never change over a recording, so the genetic score stays fixed for
  a patient. The EEG score is what moves over time. For patients carrying
  strong epilepsy genes, the model leans MORE on genetics (so their baseline
  risk floor is higher); for patients with no significant genes, it leans on
  the EEG. That is why different patient files behave differently.

==============================================================================
  HOW A FUSION FILE IS PROCESSED FOR A PREDICTION (simple explanation)
==============================================================================
If asked "what actually happens when you upload that patient file?", here is
the simple version of the pipeline:

  STEP 1 - READ THE FILE
    The uploaded patient bundle is opened and unpacked. Inside it has:
      - the patient's genetic profile (their epilepsy-gene risk score), and
      - a sequence of 30-second EEG windows with the brain-signal reading
        for each window.

  STEP 2 - SCORE EACH WINDOW WITH BOTH BRANCHES
    For every 30-second window:
      - the EEG branch gives a "how seizure-like is this brain activity right
        now" score, and
      - the genetic branch gives the patient's standing genetic risk score
        (the same value every window, because genes don't change).

  STEP 3 - THE ATTENTION GATE COMBINES THEM
    The fusion model decides how much to trust each side using a weight called
    "alpha". Roughly:

        Final risk  =  alpha x (EEG score)  +  (1 - alpha) x (Genetic score)

    If the patient carries strong epilepsy genes, the model puts more weight on
    genetics; if not, it trusts the EEG more.

  STEP 4 - TURN THE SCORE INTO AN ALERT
    The final risk score (a number between 0 and 1) is mapped to the 4-level
    clinical alert:
        0.00 - 0.25  -> Low      (Green)
        0.25 - 0.50  -> Moderate (Yellow)
        0.50 - 0.75  -> High     (Orange)
        0.75 - 1.00  -> Critical (Red)

  STEP 5 - SHOW THE TIMELINE
    Doing this for every window produces a risk timeline across the whole
    recording, which the dashboard animates and colour-codes - so you can see
    exactly when and how strongly the model raises an alarm.


==============================================================================
  QUICK ANSWERS TO LIKELY QUESTIONS
==============================================================================
Q: Why combine EEG with genetics?
A: EEG shows what the brain is doing NOW; genetics shows the patient's
   built-in risk. Together they give a more personalised, accurate warning -
   our results show a 14.4% improvement over EEG alone.

Q: What is the "attention gate" / alpha?
A: A learned weight that decides, per patient, how much to rely on EEG vs
   genetics. High-risk genes -> trust genetics more.

Q: What do the alert colours mean?
A: Green = low risk, Yellow = watch, Orange = high risk / pre-seizure,
   Red = critical. They map directly to the final fused risk score.

Q: Is the prediction live?
A: Yes - when a patient fusion file is uploaded, the dashboard processes it
   window by window and builds the risk timeline you see on screen.

Q: What datasets are behind it?
A: CHB-MIT scalp EEG (brain signals) plus ClinVar, gnomAD and GWAS (genetics).


==============================================================================
  OTHER THINGS WORTH KNOWING (good background for questions)
==============================================================================

THE THREE STATES THE MODEL LOOKS FOR
  - Interictal = normal brain activity, far from any seizure.
  - Preictal   = the build-up window BEFORE a seizure. This is the valuable
                 one - catching it is "prediction" (an early warning).
  - Ictal      = the seizure itself. Catching it is "detection".
  The goal of the project is mainly PREDICTION - warning early enough to act.

THE PREDICTION HORIZON (why this is "early warning", not just detection)
  We label the 30 minutes before a seizure as the pre-seizure window, with a
  5-minute safety gap before onset. In plain terms: the system aims to raise
  an alarm with enough lead time for a patient or carer to react, not after the
  seizure has already started.

THE TWO MODELS, IN ONE LINE EACH
  - EEG branch: a "Bidirectional LSTM with attention" - a neural network good
    at finding patterns in time-series signals; the attention part lets it
    focus on the most important moments in each 30-second window.
  - Genetic branch: an "XGBoost" model - a strong, classic machine-learning
    method that works very well on structured number tables like our 22-feature
    genetic profile.

WHAT THE HEADLINE NUMBERS ACTUALLY MEAN
  - "AUC 0.888" (fusion): a measure of how well the model separates risky from
    non-risky cases. 1.0 is perfect, 0.5 is random guessing. 0.888 is strong.
  - "+14.4% over EEG-only": fusion is clearly better than brain signals alone -
    this is the core result that justifies the whole project.
  - EEG model on its own: ~96% accuracy, catches ~89% of actual seizures and
    ~73% of pre-seizure windows.
  - In direct comparison on the test set, fusion caught 55 MORE seizure-related
    windows while also raising 61 FEWER false alarms than EEG alone - better on
    both fronts at once.

WHY WE FOCUS ON "AUC-PR" AND RECALL, NOT JUST ACCURACY
  Seizures are very rare in the data (about 96% of windows are "normal"). A lazy
  model could score 96% accuracy by always saying "no seizure" and be useless.
  So we judge the model on how well it actually catches the rare seizure cases
  (recall) and on AUC-PR, which is the fair measure when classes are imbalanced.
  This is why the dashboard highlights those metrics.

ABOUT THE GENETIC DATA (be ready for this one)
  The public EEG dataset (CHB-MIT) does NOT come with patients' DNA. So for the
  genetic side we built a large, biologically-informed synthetic patient cohort
  using real population statistics (carrier frequencies, gene-constraint scores,
  and published risk weights from the literature). It is grounded in real
  genetics, but the per-patient pairing of EEG + genes is simulated. This is a
  known, openly-stated limitation - and a natural "future work" point: validate
  on a real cohort that has both EEG and sequencing.

HONEST LIMITATIONS (saying these makes you look stronger, not weaker)
  - Genetics are synthetic/simulated, as explained above.
  - Because the synthetic genetic scores vary little, the attention gate settled
    near a balanced split rather than swinging dramatically per patient; the
    benefit still comes through clearly in the combined score.
  - Results are on the CHB-MIT patient set; a larger, multi-hospital dataset
    would be the next validation step.

WHY THIS MATTERS (the real-world value)
  - Personalisation: two patients with the same brain wave can have very
    different underlying risk because of their genes. Fusion captures that.
  - Early warning: predicting the pre-seizure window could let patients take
    precautions or medication before a seizure hits.
  - A clear, colour-coded 4-level alert is something a non-expert (a patient,
    a carer, a nurse) can actually act on.

WHERE THIS COULD GO NEXT (future work)
  - Validate on a real cohort that has both EEG and genetic sequencing.
  - Add more patients and more seizure types.
  - Wrap it into a real-time bedside / wearable monitoring tool.
